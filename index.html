Method

Endarkened Co-Composition

Listening is not extraction. It happens with, not at, or to.

EC is a collaborative, non-linear sound and storytelling methodology. It treats voices, field sounds, silences and shared experience as knowledge rather than as data to be extracted, and holds opacity and refusal as rights.

It comes from Black and Indigenous storytelling epistemologies, through Cynthia Dillard’s and S. R. Toliver’s work on endarkened knowing.
